About

Mohammad Dawood Saddiqi is a software developer and a lecturer at Ghalib University.

Mohammad Dawood is in charge of Ghalib Software Service Company, the software house of Ghalib University, and is the owner of Cyber System Software House. Mohammad Dawood designs and builds software products, databases, management systems, websites and applications, using AI and agentic AI tools to turn real needs into reliable, well-documented software.

From 2012 to 2023 Mohammad Dawood taught English at institutes and academies including AIL, AIELS, Barez and Tolo. Today Mohammad Dawood is also a web trainer in the NetCaf programmes of Netlinks, and writes books, technical guides and teaching slides.

Hesab-o-Ketab

Android app

A private, offline manager for money, projects, debts, tasks, reminders and notes, in Dari, Pashto and English.

  • Income, expenses and project accounts
  • Debts with installments and reminders
  • Tasks, reminders and a Solar Hijri or Gregorian calendar
  • Reports as PDF, CSV and Excel
  • PIN and fingerprint lock, and password-protected backups
  • Works fully offline: your records stay on your phone, apart from files you export and Android's encrypted Google backup

Version 1.1.1 is ready; its Google Play release is being prepared
